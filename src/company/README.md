# company

The business subjects of the site: the foam producer's public pages, each section a box with its door.

- `hero/`: the title banner.
- `advances/`: the four reasons to trust the producer.
- `production/`: the list of products made from foam, with pictures.
- `faq/`: the product offer, as an accordion.
- `location/`: contacts, working hours and the map of the production site.
